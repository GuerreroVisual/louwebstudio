Our Story

More than a haircut

The Sharp Edge started in 2018 with one chair and a simple idea: give people a place where they can relax, look great, and feel even better.

Today we're a team of three barbers who take pride in every cut. We don't rush, we don't cut corners (pun intended), and we always make sure you leave looking sharp.

Whether it's your first time or your fiftieth, you're family here.
