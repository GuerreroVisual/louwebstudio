WHERE ART MEETS SKIN

Pinnacle Tattoo is Corpus Christi's premier custom tattoo studio. Every piece we create is designed specifically for you — no flash, no shortcuts, no compromises.

Our artists specialize in a wide range of styles, from color realism to black and gray, neo-traditional to Japanese, and everything in between. Walk-ins are always welcome.

We also offer professional piercings with the highest quality jewelry and sterile techniques.
